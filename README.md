# LocalNews by Meenai

LocalNews by Meenai is a city-first news discovery platform that brings together local news from 300+ cities across India in one place. It helps users quickly find fresh updates from their city in both Hindi and English. [web:1][page:1]

## Features

- Browse local news from 300+ Indian cities. [web:1][page:1]
- Explore coverage state-wise across India, including Rajasthan and many other states. [page:1]
- Read city-specific pages such as Bikaner, Jaipur, Udaipur, Pune, and more. [web:2][web:3][web:5][web:6]
- Switch between Hindi and English where available. [page:2]
- Open the original publisher link for every story to read the full report at the source. [web:2][page:2]
- News updates are gathered continuously, and users can fetch fresh news for immediate refresh. [page:2]

## Why this project

LocalNews is built to make local journalism easier to discover. Instead of searching multiple websites manually, users can visit one platform and access relevant city news in a simple format. [web:1][page:2]

## Example use cases

- Check today's local updates for your city.
- Follow civic, crime, weather, business, and sports news in one place. [web:2][web:6]
- Compare local coverage across different cities in the same state. [page:1]

## Supported navigation

- Homepage for all cities and states. [web:1][page:1]
- City pages like `/news/bikaner/en` for English local coverage. [web:6][page:2]
- Language switching for Hindi and English reading experience. [page:2]
